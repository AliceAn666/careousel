# Careousel product requirements — proposal alignment

## Patient search-first redesign — October 17, 2026

This user-approved UX requirement supersedes all earlier instructions to require first-visit symptom intake or use a monthly clinic-icon calendar for patient search. No symptom questionnaire is shown in normal patient UI, including optional onboarding. No intake-completion gate applies to search, direct booking, waitlist registration, acceptance or guides. Existing Intake/SymptomTag rows and historical API compatibility are retained without deletion, migration or automatic backfill. Clinician-authored tags are implemented in Phase 8 Part A; patient-authorized synthetic record import and clinician-reviewed extraction are implemented in Part B. Do not invent clinical information.

Patient navigation centers on Book an Appointment, Find an Earlier Appointment, and Manage My Appointments and Waitlists, plus the durable notification inbox. Search includes a service/specialty text field, categories (Vaccinations, Blood Tests, Routine Checkups mapped to Primary care / Routine examination, Specialist Visits), service/location/insurance/date/listed-cost and fictional estimated-patient-cost filters. Only matching labeled appointment cards appear, earliest first; details/booking and waitlist actions remain available. Vaccination, Blood test and Routine examination are supported request service strings, but current synthetic fixtures may have no matching openings; display an honest empty state. Never relabel a specialist appointment as another service. Clinic operational schedules are preserved.

Service-specific referral/authorization, saved-plan eligibility, availability, request/clinic human-approved P1–P4, account/clinic scope, atomic replacement, occupancy constraints and authorized automatic 180-minute group sequences remain enforced. No shared database reset/reseed, automatic migration, clinical inference or real insurance-verification claim.

Acceptance: new patients can browse/book/join/accept without Intake rows; historical intake survives normal operations; search results combine filters, show readable clinic/service/location/time/price, exclude nonmatches and sort chronologically; empty categories allow waitlist registration; navigation/home/refresh preserve account and bookings; existing clinic/group/concurrency/expiry/rollback regressions continue passing in isolation.

## Phase 7 — current authoritative notification policy

This section supersedes ALL older internal-clinic-first/release-stage plans and requirements for a separate manual send per group below. Historical proposal comparisons and validation records are retained as history, not active acceptance criteria. Base: PR #13 merged main `0de5aaaf175b10fe86bed5cb7c8cbaa44e598192`; [main CI 39013494376](https://github.com/AliceAn666/careousel/actions/runs/39013494376) succeeded before the feature branch.

- One shared cross-clinic eligible candidate pool, with no internal-clinic-first preference, window or extra network release stage.
- Exact P1 Time-Sensitive, P2 High Priority, P3 Routine, P4 Flexible, human-approved for each request at the offering clinic. No missing-level default, patient-assigned level or AI clinical inference.
- Staff preview eligibility/exclusions, explicitly select recipients and the first group, and authorize the complete sequence once. The first eligible selected group sends immediately. Only the saved recipient snapshot can be sent automatically later; newly arriving/changed patients need explicit review.
- Next actual group send is at least **180 minutes after the previous server-recorded actual send**, even after restart, delay, cancellation/restart or pause/resume. Empty/invalid groups are skipped without send timestamps or interval reset. Delayed processing sends one group, then schedules from that actual send; it never catches up in bursts.
- Durable PostgreSQL sequences/groups, independent supervised worker (`npm run worker:notifications`), transaction-scoped advisory lock and idempotent persisted group status. No browser or read request drives automatic sends. Worker outage delays delivery; restart safely recovers overdue work. Do not confuse durable scheduling with a guarantee of exact wall-clock delivery.
- All sent groups compete for one slot. Group invitations expire after 12 hours or appointment start, whichever is earlier; prior unexpired invitations remain valid when the next group sends. Legacy exclusive invitations retain their ten-minute hold and cannot mix with group invitations.
- Successful committed acceptance atomically books, safely replaces only a linked original appointment, fulfills that request, terminates the sequence, cancels future groups, revokes competing invitations and persists audit/notifications. Failed acceptance rolls back and leaves scheduling/original bookings intact. Direct booking cannot bypass active/paused/scheduled groups or pending competitive invitations.
- Changed request/priority snapshot pauses the sequence for explicit reauthorization of remaining groups. Inactive/withdrawn/ineligible recipients are skipped, never replaced automatically. Revoked author authority pauses; revoked priority-assigner authority excludes affected recipients. Staff receive durable in-app warnings and audit entries. Pause stops future sends but does not revoke invitations already sent; Cancel does revoke them. Resume reuses the same approved snapshot; fresh preview + authorization can replace only unsent groups, preserving prior send times/history.
- Slot referral/authorization requirements fail closed until staff explicitly approve synthetic request/clinic clearance; accept rechecks it. Existing low-complexity slots default to not requiring these flags. No claim of actual insurer/referral verification or clinical screening.

## 2. Product and practical architecture

Careousel is an authorized cancellation-recovery network complementing clinics' existing scheduling processes. A patient manages multiple continuing care needs in one account. A clinic first offers suitable openings internally, then explicitly releases unused capacity to participating authorized clinics' patients. Staff control eligibility review, notification groups and scheduling decisions. Success is a durable, demonstrable recovery workflow; clinical benefit and commercial viability remain hypotheses.

Keep the existing Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, original responsive CSS/Lucide, PostgreSQL, Prisma 6.19.2 with pg adapter, Zod, npm lockfile, Vitest and Playwright. Keep one application and one database. Do not migrate frameworks or restore the deleted static frontend. Persistent inbox and polling remain adequate for a short hackathon; no paid AI/API or message provider is needed. One PostgreSQL advisory lock is an acceptable demo throughput tradeoff. Thin route handlers, domain services and database constraints remain the boundaries.

Use integer cents with USD explicitly displayed, UTC persistence and clinic IANA timezone display. Current geography is one US metro with Pacific-time synthetic clinics. City/clinic selection is functional; distance routing/geocoding is deferred. Production identity assurance, EHR synchronization and real-time external hospital availability are not implemented. Basic individual prototype accounts are implemented in the R3/R4 scope below.

## 4. Scheduling protection audit and integration risk

Current `performAction()` samples `new Date()` (or a test clock) inside the advisory-locked callback, before validation. This fixes the original pre-lock timestamp placement; it does **not** use PostgreSQL `clock_timestamp()`. The lock-wait regression advances an injected clock, not a real offer expiry observed by the database. Add a database clock sampled after lock acquisition (never transaction-start `now()`), plus real lock-wait tests for acceptance, direct booking and staff approval. Preserve lock 728194 and current timezone tests.

Remote cancellation commits before matching in a second locked transaction; the catch preserves cancellation and later reads retry derived suggestions. However, `getState()` still runs refreshMatches without an error boundary, so persistent matching failure can break the entire snapshot/UI. The injected failure occurs before refreshMatches rather than exercising an aborted SQL transaction. Test database failure, recoverable reads and explicit idempotent staff retry. Matching must never generate notifications automatically. Acceptance must remain one atomic transaction; do not apply cancellation's split-transaction pattern to replacement.

Current `0002` provides original Booking.replacementId→successor, patient-scoped composite foreign keys for Waitlist→Booking and Offer→Waitlist, plus a no-self/status check. Notification→Offer is not patient-scoped; slot status versus simultaneous booking/offer occupancy and raw-SQL patient overlap are not fully enforced by separate partial indexes. Add targeted SQL integrity tests and practical constraints only after data preflight.

Local-only review work uses the reverse `replacedBookingId` relation and different migrations named `0002_reschedule_history`/`0003_scheduling_integrity`; it also contains stronger SQL-clock, read-failure and integrity tests. **Do not combine these histories or apply both 0002 designs blindly.** T0 must reconcile useful behavior/tests onto the remote schema, preserve applied migration checksums and keep original local work untouched until separately reviewed. Remote VALIDATION reports its 0002 was applied in its environment; that does not establish which migrations exist on another teammate's machine.

Remote acceptance revokes all pending offers for a patient, including unrelated service needs. Multi-need support must revoke only same-need or genuinely conflicting offers, with concurrency tests. Existing service-string uniqueness must not be removed until stable need-level deduplication replaces it.

## 6. Backend transitions and scheduling contract

Occupancy and release phase are independent: Slot AVAILABLE/HELD/BOOKED continues to represent capacity; OpeningRelease INTERNAL_ONLY/EXTERNAL_REVIEW/NETWORK_OPEN/CLOSED represents audience permission. Patient search and **all direct-booking paths** must obey this audience gate, not merely the candidate list.

1. Cancellation transaction validates actor, changes booking to CANCELLED, releases occupancy, writes internal release deadline/policy snapshot and audit/event. If the cancellation itself fails, all these changes roll back and the UI reports failure. Once committed, matching failure cannot undo it; return matchingPending and offer safe retry. Ordinary new openings also begin internal-first unless the reviewed policy explicitly permits a different authorized path.
2. Derived matching checks active need/enrollment, internal relationship or active network memberships plus patient opt-in, service specification/duration, specific demo plan, target-clinic referral/auth validity, attendance lead time/preferences, price, earlier-only link and patient conflicts. Unknown required evidence means ineligible with a missing-information reason, not silent approval. Revalidate at invitation and acceptance.
3. Before internalUntil, only the clinic's own enrolled patients can receive offers. At/after deadline, INTERNAL_ONLY becomes EXTERNAL_REVIEW; deadline passage alone never authorizes external sharing. Staff explicitly releases to NETWORK_OPEN after confirming no successful internal fill, remaining holds resolved and permitted network. Membership/consent revocation invalidates affected pending network offers. An empty candidate set stays a visible no-match opening.
4. Clinician markers are pre-existing human inputs. Staff selects a qualified cohort, records rationale and approves its stage. Equal marker/cohort candidates use createdAt then stable ID. The October 17 update below replaces the earlier sequential-exclusive proposal: staff explicitly sends a reviewed P1–P4 group simultaneously, with at least 180 minutes between sends. Multiple invitations compete for one seat, never represent independent seat reservations, and transactional first-valid-confirmation is an administrative capacity rule, not clinical priority.
5. Current legacy staff approval atomically creates one PENDING offer, HELD slot and durable inbox entry/event. Planned group send instead creates one authorized sequence/group and its recipient offers/notices atomically, under the updated occupancy contract below. expiresAt=min(executionTime+clinic offer duration, slot.start−required arrival lead time); reject nonpositive remaining windows. Snapshot policy and eligibility versions; later changes cannot silently extend a hold.
6. Patient accept, under the same scheduling lock, obtains database execution time, revalidates ownership, unexpired state, release/network permission and all hard eligibility. In **one transaction**, create new confirmed booking, link and mark old RESCHEDULED, release old slot into its own internal process, fulfill only this need, revoke conflicting/same-need offers, confirm occupancy and persist notification/events. Failure rolls back everything and protects the old appointment. No external cancellation is claimed.
7. Legacy decline/expiry releases its exclusive hold and preserves original booking and still-valid need. In group mode, decline/expiry affects only that offer; release the group hold only after no valid competing offers remain and do not automatically send the next group. Repeated same response succeeds without duplicate events/notifications; contradictory response returns conflict. Expiry correctness must not require a worker or browser timer. Staff can retry matching explicitly; reads degrade safely if projection fails. Reopening/renotifying requires a deliberate new stage, not an accidental retry.

For a hackathon, lazy reconciliation under the lock plus UI polling is acceptable. Timed stages become eligible by server deadline and staff action; do not promise background delivery at an exact second. Durable stage/release rows allow restart recovery. A scheduler can be added later without changing rules.

### R2 — manually authorized group invitations, three hours apart

Staff preview eligible requests grouped by approved P1–P4 for a specific opening, review exclusions and approve a recipient set, then explicitly send the approved group in one transaction. Persist an audience snapshot/version and recheck every recipient at send and accept. Simultaneous means all durable in-app invitations in the same committed send operation, not guaranteed simultaneous external message delivery. Proposed model: OpeningNotificationSequence(id, slotId, releaseGeneration, status, version); NotificationGroup(sequenceId, priorityLevel, status, approvedBy/At, sentBy/At, notBefore, operationKey, eligibilityVersion); GroupRecipient(groupId, waitlistId, patientId, approvedBy/At, eligibilitySnapshot, exclusionReason); Offer.groupId plus unique(groupId, waitlistId). Eligibility errors are shown before sending; if any approved recipient becomes ineligible, reject the send without partial delivery and require review. An empty group is explicitly SKIPPED with audit, sends no invitations and does not restart the interval. Duplicate retry with the same operationKey returns the committed result, not another wave.

First approved eligible group can be sent immediately after applicable internal-release rules. Subsequent actually sent groups require executionTime >= lastSentAt + 180 minutes, regardless of prior declines or expiry. The timer never sends automatically. Preview, polling, workers, service restarts, expired invitations and patient responses can reconcile state but cannot send another group. A later send always needs a currently authorized clinic actor. P1→P2→P3→P4 is the default progression; missing/unapproved higher groups require an explicit skip rationale. Changes to priority after approval invalidate the affected draft review; a sent cohort retains its historical level snapshot and any pending acceptance must still satisfy current administrative authorization and eligibility.

Show last send, next eligible send, server-derived countdown, per-group status/recipient eligibility/exclusions, remaining capacity and offer expiry separately. If a subsequent group's earliest send would leave no valid acceptance/arrival window, show a warning and make that group unsendable at the boundary. No silent shortened interval or automatic urgency override. Controlled clocks in tests can simulate hours; the normal demo still enforces 180 minutes. Internal/external audience transitions cannot reset the clock for the same release; cancellation/reopening creates an audited new release generation rather than bypassing a live sequence.

Current Slot represents one appointment seat; retain capacity=1 for the first implementation. Remaining capacity is 1 minus confirmed occupancy. A pending group offer is an invitation, not an exclusive per-recipient seat reservation. Retain legacy exclusive offers during migration; never mix a legacy exclusive hold and a competitive group sequence for one slot. Before relaxing one_pending_offer_per_slot, introduce and test the replacement invariant: multiple pending offers only in the authorized sequence for that slot, at most one CONFIRMED booking, no booked slot with remaining actionable competitor offers. A HELD group slot may contain multiple pending offers, but total confirmed occupancy remains one. If future capacity>1 is requested, model distinct seats or a separate transactional capacity design; do not pretend current schema supports it.

Acceptance acquires existing serialization locks, samples post-lock authoritative execution time, rechecks identity, membership, eligibility, expiry and remaining capacity, then atomically books, links/reschedules the old booking, fulfills the request, writes notifications/history and invalidates competing offers. First valid acceptance fills the seat and closes the sequence; later acceptances conflict safely. A failed acceptance rolls back fully and leaves the original booking confirmed. Decline/expiry affects only that invitation; do not release a group hold while other valid offers remain. Cancellation must remain committed if derived matching fails. No-match openings stay visible. Direct-book, legacy approve, cancel, withdraw, preference edits, expiry and retry paths must share the same occupancy rules. Original appointments remain protected through every invitation, decline, failed accept and competitor win.
