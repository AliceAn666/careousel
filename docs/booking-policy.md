# Final appointment and waitlist policy

The shared `src/lib/service-eligibility.ts` allowlist contains exact canonical services: **Vaccination**, **Blood test**, **Routine examination**. All other services, including Dermatology, Cardiology and Primary care consultations, require clinic-reviewed invitations. Never classify by substring, symptoms, icon or an alias. No schema or data migration is needed.

`POST /api/actions` has two distinct booking-producing paths. `book` checks the persisted Slot.service under the scheduling transaction before any confirmed booking can be created, including requests with needId and self-pay acknowledgment. Restricted services fail with HTTP 403 and a specific shared-waitlist explanation. `respond` independently validates a valid staff-approved invitation, all eligibility and expiry/ownership/occupancy checks, then can confirm any supported service. `/api/patient-needs` accepts need management actions only; actual need-specific booking goes through the guarded `book` action. No other public confirmed-booking endpoint exists.

Basic direct care retains saved-insurer/demo self-pay, referral/authorization clearance, appointment overlap, availability, authorized notification sequence and concurrent-booking protections. Required clearance is never fabricated. Patients are charged no real payment; prices/insurance are synthetic demonstrations.

Patient search lists service/clinic information for specialists with Join Waitlist / Find an Earlier Appointment, without ordinary appointment-time controls. Basic care retains location, service, clinic, listed-price, insurance compatibility and availability browsing; warnings keep potentially incompatible clinics visible. Need-specific browsing also hides restricted appointment selectors and guides the patient to that need's waitlist. Homepage, entry, navigation and optional tour explain both pathways. No symptom questionnaire is added.

There is **one universal cross-clinic candidate pool**. No offering-clinic patient boost, internal waiting/exhaustion requirement, home-clinic priority, separate network queue or external release exists. Eligibility uses service, insurance, location, scheduling and appointment constraints. Authorized staff select final P1–P4, recipients and initiate notification sequences. Later groups advance automatically at least 180 minutes after the previous actual send; successful fill terminates the sequence. AI and symptoms never assign clinical urgency.

Patients can join without an original appointment, preserve a confirmed internal original until an atomic replacement succeeds, register unverified external hospital appointments, and manage independent same-service needs. External cancellation remains the patient's responsibility and is explicitly explained. Acceptance changes only the linked need and slot competitors; unrelated bookings/requests survive. Original failures, expiry and concurrency rollback remain protected.

Verification uses synthetic fixtures in a fresh isolated PostgreSQL careousel_test database. Historical tests that directly booked specialist fixtures are adapted to basic-service fixtures or explicit historical booking setup; specialist invitation regressions remain specialist workflows. See VALIDATION.md for actual checks and remaining limitations. No shared reset/reseed, real hospital/insurance connection, migration, auto-merge or deployment.
